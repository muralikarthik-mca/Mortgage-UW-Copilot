# DEMONSTRATION POLICIES — FICTIONAL; NOT LENDING GUIDANCE

These rules are created solely to exercise a document-grounded RAG system. They are not Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, FHA, or lender policies. Do not use them for real loans.

## TR-INC-01 — Base income review
For the fictional training workflow, a reviewer considers a recent pay statement and prior-year wage documentation when assessing salaried borrowers. Alternative evidence may be acceptable, and an underwriter determines the appropriate path. A provided monthly income value is unverified until reviewed.

## TR-INC-02 — Pay statement recency
The fictional demo process flags a pay statement that is more than 60 calendar days before the loan's application date for manual review. This is a made-up threshold for testing date comparisons; do not attribute it to any agency.

## TR-INC-03 — Employment evidence
The fictional demo process records whether employment verification is present or absent. Absence alone is not evidence of noncompliance because documentation paths vary. Show a conditional follow-up, not an automatic failure.

## TR-INC-04 — Bonus income
A fictional borrower with bonus income should have additional earnings-history evidence examined before bonus amounts are considered verified. The assistant should request source history if none is present and should not infer a qualifying amount.

## TR-INC-05 — Multiple jobs
For fictional borrowers reporting more than one employer, the reviewer considers the evidence for each separate income source. Missing evidence for one source must not invalidate documented income from another source automatically.

## TR-INC-06 — Self-employment
A fictional self-employment case requires specialist review of business and personal tax information before qualifying income can be established. The demo does not make a final approval recommendation.

## TR-INC-07 — Evidence and outcome
Findings must cite both retrieved policy text and loan-specific source records. Missing or conflicting evidence must be labeled 'Needs review' or 'Insufficient evidence'. Only an authorized human may decide final eligibility.
